NOW LOOK AGAIN

What changed when one fragment changed? Which version felt more clinically plausible — and why?

This is not a diagnostic tool. It is a toy-sized prompt for thinking about how clinicians assemble a patient from partial information: observations, shorthand, context, interpretation and assumptions.

Nothing generated here is intended to form a coherent or realistic case. The joins are the point.
